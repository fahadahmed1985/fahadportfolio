# Content Generation System

This document explains how video ideas, scripts, and metadata are generated before entering the automation pipeline.

The goal is to generate **hundreds or thousands of video concepts quickly** while maintaining consistency and viral potential.

---

# Content Production Philosophy

The system focuses on three proven motivational content styles.

A) Stoic Wisdom  
B) High Energy Hustle Motivation  
C) Life Advice Storytelling

These styles perform well for motivational Shorts because they are:

• easy to understand  
• emotionally engaging  
• short-form friendly  

---

# Content Structure

Every video follows a consistent storytelling pattern.

Hook  
↓  
Insight  
↓  
Reinforcement  
↓  
Closing Line

Typical length:

15–20 seconds

---

# Example Script Structure

Hook:

Discipline is the highest form of self-respect.

Body:

Every promise you keep to yourself builds confidence.  
Small daily actions create powerful habits.  
Those habits shape the life you want.

Closing:

Stay consistent. Your future self is watching.

# Generating Large Volumes of Content

Content ideas can be generated using:

ChatGPT

Typical generation batches:

20 videos  
50 videos  
100 videos  

Once generated, ideas are added to the spreadsheet and processed automatically.

---

# Hook Patterns

Hooks determine whether viewers stop scrolling.

Effective hook patterns include:

Truth statements

Example  
"Most people fail because they quit too early."

Contrarian insight

Example  
"Motivation is not what creates success."

Curiosity

Example  
"What happens when you focus for one year?"

Short motivational command

Example  
"Stop negotiating with your goals."

---

# Title Optimization

Titles should be:

• short  
• emotionally engaging  
• curiosity driven  

Example titles:

Consistency Beats Talent  
Your Future Self Is Watching  
Pressure Creates Diamonds  
Stop Waiting For Motivation  

---

# Hashtag Strategy

Use a small set of strong hashtags.

Example:

#motivation  
#mindset  
#success  
#discipline  
#selfimprovement  

Avoid excessive hashtags.

---

# Visual Scene Prompts

Each video includes simple scene prompts to guide background visuals.

Example:

1 sunrise runner tying shoes  
2 athlete training alone  
3 focused work late night  
4 standing on mountain summit

These prompts are used to choose matching background footage.

---

# Batch Content Strategy

Content is produced in batches.

Example workflow:

Generate 20 ideas  
↓  
Generate scripts  
↓  
Add metadata  
↓  
Run automation pipeline  

This allows rapid scaling.
